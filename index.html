My Contacts App

This is a portable desktop application that allows you to view, manage, and synchronize your Google Contacts.

It reads your contacts via the Google People API and stores them locally in a secure, encrypted database.

Features:

  • View and search contacts
  • Synchronize with Google Contacts
  • Import from VCF files
  • Export to Word documents
