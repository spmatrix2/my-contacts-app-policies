Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23-02-2026

Thank you for using My Contacts App ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

The App accesses your Google Contacts solely to provide its core functionality: viewing, managing, and synchronizing your contacts locally. We collect the following types of information:

     
  • Contact Data: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, organizations, and any other information stored in your Google Contacts.
    •  
  • Account Information: To link your Google account, we use a secure token. Your password is never stored. The token is encrypted and stored locally on your device, accessible only by you.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to provide the App's features:

     
  • To display your contacts within the App.
    •  
  • To allow you to search and organize your contacts.
    •  
  • To synchronize your contacts with your Google account as per your request.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

All contact data is stored locally in a SQLite database on the device where the App is installed. The OAuth token used to access your Google account is encrypted and stored within this local database. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

4. Your Rights and Control

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions page. You may also delete the App and its local database from your device, which will remove all locally stored data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
